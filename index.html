GOOD TO KNOW

A few practical details.

Does the Android app work offline?

The current Android 0.13 release uses local tools and bundled speech audio. Opening a website or the Google Play listing uses your external browser.

Can I use Floating Mode?

Floating Mode is optional and requires Android’s “Display over other apps” permission. Drag the panel, close it or expand back to the app.

How do alarms work?

Android permissions allow notifications, scheduled alerts and supported full-screen alerts. Power saving, force-stop settings and device policies can affect delivery. Check alarm permissions and test an alert on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Tool state, chosen cities and preferences are stored on your device. The current Android release has no account system or analytics SDK. See the Android Privacy Policy.

Is the Android app the same as the website?

They are separate products. The Android app includes native alerts and an optional floating panel; the website provides browser-based tools with browser permissions and limitations.
