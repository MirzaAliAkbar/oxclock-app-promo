Android App Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

App: OxClock (com.oxclock.pocket), version 0.13

OxClock is an offline clock, alarm and time-tools app. This policy describes the Android app; websites opened in your browser have their own policies.

Data collection and sharing

This release does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer and does not share personal data with third parties. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK, no account system, no cloud speech service and no active paid subscription. Ad Preview and Coming Soon are interface previews, not live advertising or purchases. The app does not request internet, microphone, camera, contacts, location or shared-photo/storage access.

Local information

Your selected cities, alarms, timer and cycle settings, running-tool state, stopwatch laps, counter value and preferences are stored privately on your device. Time speech is bundled offline audio; a selected audio file may be copied into the app's private cache for playback. Speech waves use precomputed levels, not microphone recordings. These files and settings are not uploaded. Android app backup is disabled for this release.

Permissions

Notifications show alerts and floating-mode status, with Stop/Snooze or Close actions. Exact alarms schedule your requested alarms and timers. Full-screen alerts may open the relevant tool for an alarm where Android permits it. Display over other apps lets you start a draggable floating panel. Foreground services support alarm sound and the user-selected floating panel. Wake lock supports ringing, vibration supports feedback and alerts, and boot/time-change receivers restore schedules. Floating mode is optional; you can dismiss it from its notification. Android permissions can be changed in system Settings. Denying a permission may limit the related feature.

Retention and deletion

Local settings remain until changed, the app's storage is cleared in Android Settings, or the app is uninstalled. Clearing storage or uninstalling also removes your saved settings and schedules. Reset All resets tools but intentionally keeps settings and cities. Temporary speech cache is cleaned after playback where possible; clearing app storage removes remaining cache.

External services

Opening OxClock's website or a contact link launches an external browser/email app at your request. The app does not send saved tool data with these links. Those services may process information under their own policies. Google Play and Android may handle installation, purchases on other apps, diagnostics or other platform data independently of OxClock; consult their policies.

Support

If you email us, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive information. Support messages are kept only as needed to handle the request and normal correspondence; ask by email to delete correspondence where feasible. Support email: mirza.khi.pk@gmail.com

Website: https://oxclock.com/contact

Children

OxClock is a general time utility, not a service designed to collect information from children. This release collects no personal data through the app.

Changes

If a future release adds data collection, online speech, advertising or billing, its policy and Google Play declarations will be updated before release.

This promotional website

app.oxclock.com is a separate static promotional website. It has no analytics, advertising SDK, account forms or application-data collection. The host necessarily receives requests, IP addresses and browser information to deliver and secure pages. Opening a Google Play, email or main OxClock website link brings you to that service and its own policy. The main OxClock website uses Google Analytics; see its separate policy.
